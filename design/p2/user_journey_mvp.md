# User Journey

### Swiping through her feed and Deciding with a First Impression card

The app leads Jane to her feed where she is instructed to click the right button (Up Next) if she likes a book and left (Pass) otherwise. The app then shows her one book at a time, the app calls each book card a First Impression card. The first book is Brave New World, the radar chart shows that it scores very highly on social commentary and dystopian. She reads through the spoiler-free summary, characters, themes, and tones. She decides it doesn't pique her interest and she swipes left on this book. A few swipes later the app shows her The Giver. She decides to swipe right on this book, and the app makes it easy to see that the book is a good match with the radar chart ranking this book high introspective.

### Looking through her Up Next Shelf

After swiping through some books, Jane decides to take a look at her shelf. When she clicks on her shelf she sees a list of books marked as "Up Next" which were all the books that she swiped right on. From this list, she decides that The Giver will be her next read. She then decides to go to the library the next day, and checks out The Giver. After a week and a half, she finishes the book! She really liked the book and feels like she has a better understanding of what she is looking for in a new book.

### Favoriting books and choose your next read

Since Jane finished The Giver, she decides to add this book to her list of favorites since she really liked it. Jane does this by going to her Up Next list and clicking the "Finished: add to Favorites" button. Instead of the familiar dread of having to choose her next book, she opens up her feed for new book suggestions or opens up her Up Next list to see books that pique her interest.

### Outcome

Jane never has to stand in front of a library shelf feeling lost and overwhelmed again. The app didn't pick her books for her, instead it gave her clear, comprehensive information about each book so that she can be exposed to books that match her criteria.
