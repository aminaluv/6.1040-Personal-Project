# Application Pitch

## Name
Novelty

## Motivation
Whether someone is trying to get into reading for the first time, returning after a while, searching for a book that fits exactly what they want, or finishing a great book with nothing lined up, finding the right book is hard. The struggle to choose a great next read can lead to wasted time, abandoned books, and reading habits that never start or quickly fall apart. Many new and returning readers freeze in front of endless choices, waste hours on recommendations that don't fit, or stop reading before a habit can take hold.

## Key Features

### First Impressions
Many book apps ask you to decide whether you like a book from the book cover and a short summary of the book. With Novelty, every book gets a card showing what actually matters. This card includes the book's genre, author, published date, setting, characters' age range, a short summary of the book, and a radar chart that scores the book based on **your** definition of a good book such as pacing, character depth, or humor. You can define your criteria of what you consider to be a good book in your reader profile. A reader that is looking for a "sci-fi book with character that are my age" can tell in seconds whether a book matches their specifications rather than spending hours cross-checking reviews and Reddit, all just to end up with a book that they never finish. With Novelty, readers can choose new books with confidence and lesser-known books get a fair look based on content rather than hype. 

### Meet Cute Matchmaker
This is a personalized feed of First Impressions based on what you have read and loved as well as the criteria you defined in your reader profile. Swipe right to save a book to your "Up Next" list, or swipe left to pass. Every swipe makes future book suggestions smarter and more personalized. Seeing one book at a time reduces the paralysis that comes with entering a crowded library with a yes or no decision. The Up Next list builds as you swipe right, so your next read is lined up even before you finish your current read. This allows the user to keep up a reading habit even at the moment it usually breaks, and this gives the reader a low-pressure, exciting way to keep reading. 


### Shared Shelves
Shared Shelves allow the reader to follow friends to see the books that they have saved, finished, and loved. Seeing book picks from someone that you know is much more trustworthy than a stranger's opinion, and seeing a friend's shelf makes browsing social instead of a chore. Readers can get recommendations that they trust, friends can talk about books that they have on their Up Next list, and avid readers that love to browse can share their book discoveries with others.
